Questions about Sivra

What is Sivra?

Sivra is a daily AI fluency app. It helps users build practical judgment through briefings, drills, and written articulation practice.

Do I need an account?

V1 uses anonymous app access. No email or password is required to begin.

What is included in Sivra Pro?

Sivra Pro unlocks AI-generated daily packs when the backend AI service is available. Free users can continue with curated packs.

How do I get support?

Email support@veloranlabs.com.
